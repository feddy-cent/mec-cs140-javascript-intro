🍕 Homemade Pizza Recipe

        

            Welcome to my homemade pizza recipe!             This pizza has a crispy crust, delicious tomato sauce,             and melted cheese. Follow these simple steps to make             your own pizza at home.         

                 

Ingredients

        
                
  •                                      Pizza dough                              
    •             
  •                                      Pizza sauce                              
    •             
  •                                      Mozzarella cheese                              
    •             
  •                                      Pepperoni                              
    •             
  •                                      Green bell peppers                              
    •             
  •                                      Olive oil                              
    •             
  •                                      Italian seasoning                              
    •         
        

Instructions

        
                
  1.                 Preheat your oven to 425°F (220°C).             
    2.             
  2.                 Place the pizza dough on a clean surface.                 Stretch it into a round shape to make the pizza crust.             
    3.             
  3.                 Spread the pizza sauce evenly over the dough,                 leaving a little space around the edges.             
    4.             
  4.                 Sprinkle mozzarella cheese over the sauce.             
    5.             
  5.                 Add pepperoni and chopped green bell peppers                 on top of the cheese.             
    6.             
  6.                 Brush a little olive oil around the crust                 and sprinkle Italian seasoning over the pizza.             
    7.             
  7.                 Place the pizza on a baking tray and bake it                 for about 12–15 minutes, or until the crust is                 golden brown and the cheese is melted.             
    8.             
  8.                 Carefully remove the pizza from the oven.                 Let it cool for a few minutes before cutting it                 into slices.             
    9.         
        

Enjoy Your Pizza! 😋

        

            Serve your homemade pizza with your favorite drink             or side dish. Enjoy this delicious meal with family             and friends!
