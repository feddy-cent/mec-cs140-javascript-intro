🍕 Homemade Pizza Recipe

        

            Welcome to my homemade pizza recipe!             This pizza has a crispy crust, delicious tomato sauce,             and melted cheese. Follow these easy steps to make             your own pizza at home.         

                 

Ingredients

        
                
  •                 Pizza dough             
    •             
  •                 Pizza sauce             
    •             
  •                 Mozzarella cheese             
    •             
  •                 Pepperoni             
    •             
  •                 Green bell peppers             
    •             
  •                 Olive oil             
    •         
        

Instructions

        
                
  1.                 Preheat your oven to 425°F (220°C).             
    2.             
  2.                 Place the pizza dough on a clean surface                 and stretch it into a round shape.             
    3.             
  3.                 Spread the pizza sauce evenly over the dough.             
    4.             
  4.                 Add mozzarella cheese, pepperoni,                 and chopped green bell peppers.             
    5.             
  5.                 Brush a little olive oil around the crust                 to help it become golden brown.             
    6.             
  6.                 Bake the pizza for about 12–15 minutes,                 until the crust is golden and the cheese is melted.                 Make sure the pizza is fully cooked.             
    7.             
  7.                 Carefully remove the pizza from the oven.                 Let it cool for a few minutes before slicing.             
    8.         
        

Enjoy Your Pizza! 😋

        

            Serve your homemade pizza with your favorite drink             and enjoy a delicious meal with family or friends!
